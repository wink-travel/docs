    <h3 id="ap-2-collection">2. Collection</h3>
    <p><strong>2.1</strong>  TripPay collects the Booking Value from the Guest at the time and by the method applicable to the rate plan and policy you set. Card processing is performed by Stripe, and by transacting you also agree to Stripe’s applicable terms.</p>
    <p><strong>2.2</strong>  Your name normally appears on the Guest’s card or bank statement for the Booking.</p>
    <p><strong>2.3</strong>  TripPay does not store full payment card numbers. Card data is handled by Stripe under PCI-DSS.</p>
    <p><strong>2.4</strong>  Authorisation, capture and settlement timing may vary by rate plan, policy and payment method.</p>
    <h3 id="ap-3-handling-of-funds">3. Handling of funds</h3>
    <p><strong>3.1</strong>  Funds collected from Guests are held with TripPay’s regulated payment processor until they are due for payout to you under clause 5.</p>
    <p><strong>3.2</strong>  TripPay does not invest funds collected from Guests and does not retain interest earned on them.</p>
    <p><strong>3.3</strong>  TripPay receives such funds solely for the purpose of remitting them in accordance with these Payment Terms.</p>
    <h3 id="ap-4-fees">4. Fees</h3>
    <p><strong>4.1</strong>  Payment Collection Fee. TripPay charges 4.0% of Booking Value for payment collection and settlement, inclusive of card processing.</p>
    <p><strong>4.2</strong>  Platform Fee. Wink charges 1.5% of Booking Value under the Accommodation Provider Agreement. TripPay deducts and remits it to Wink on your behalf.</p>
    <p><strong>4.3</strong>  Partner Commission. Where a Demand Partner generated the Booking, TripPay deducts the Partner Commission calculated under the Accommodation Provider Agreement and settles it to that Demand Partner.</p>

    <p><strong>5.1</strong>  The Net Rate becomes available for withdrawal within 24 hours of Guest check-in, or of delivery in the case of an Add-On, subject to successful receipt of the Guest payment and to verification under clause 7.</p>

No amount may be added to that balance other than by way of Bookings, and the only permitted use is withdrawal to your registered bank account.</p>
    <p><strong>5.3</strong>  Any amount not withdrawn within 30 days is paid automatically to your registered bank account.</p>
    <p><strong>5.4</strong>  You must provide and maintain accurate payout details. Only the designated representative named at registration may change bank or payee details, and any change must be requested in writing.</p>
    <p><strong>5.5</strong>  Where you provide incorrect payout details, any bank charge arising is deducted from your next payout. Where your account is closed, frozen or unable to receive payment for reasons not attributable to TripPay, TripPay’s payment obligation is discharged provided its records show payment was made.</p>
    <p><strong>5.6</strong>  TripPay may limit the value of a single payout for operational or compliance reasons and make a series of payouts instead.</p>
    <h3 id="ap-6-refunds-chargebacks-and-errors">6. Refunds, chargebacks and errors</h3>
    <p><strong>6.1</strong>  Refunds due to a Guest under your policy are initiated and remitted by TripPay on your behalf, and processed promptly subject to card scheme rules.</p>
    <p><strong>6.2</strong>  Chargebacks. Where a Guest successfully disputes a payment, the disputed amount is reversed against you. If it has already been paid out, it is recovered from your next payout, and any shortfall carries forward until cleared. Where the relationship ends with an amount outstanding, TripPay may invoice you for it.</p>
    <p><strong>6.3</strong>  Fraud carve-out. TripPay absorbs the disputed amount where the dispute arises from confirmed payment fraud that you could not reasonably have prevented, including use of a stolen card or a compromised Guest account. This does not apply to disputes concerning the stay itself, the application of your policies, no-shows, service quality, or the accuracy of your Supply.</p>
    <p><strong>6.4</strong>  Representment. We will notify you promptly of any dispute. You will provide the folio, registration record and any other supporting evidence within five business days. Where you do not, the disputed amount is reversed against you regardless of clause 6.3.</p>
    <p><strong>6.5</strong>  Processing errors. TripPay will correct any payment processing error, including by crediting or debiting your account. Where you receive funds in error you will return them immediately, and TripPay may recover them from future payouts.</p>
    <p><strong>6.6</strong>  You authorise TripPay to recover from you, by deduction from future payouts or by charging the payment method associated with the relevant Booking, any amount you owe under these Payment Terms or the Accommodation Provider Agreement, including refunds, reversed payouts, Taxes and fees.</p>
