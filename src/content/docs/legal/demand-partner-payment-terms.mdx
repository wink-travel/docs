## 1. What these Payment Terms do, and do not, do

| **1.1**  TripPay collects payment from Travellers as the limited payment collection agent of the Accommodation Provider, under the Accommodation Provider Payment Terms. You are not a party to that collection. **1.2**  You do not receive, hold, control or have any beneficial interest in Traveller funds at any point. You do not appoint TripPay as your agent for the collection of Traveller funds, and TripPay does not act as your agent for that purpose. **1.3**  Where a Booking is attributed to you, TripPay settles your Commission to you out of amounts it has actually received from the Traveller. That obligation is a payment obligation owed to you and is not a holding of funds on your behalf. **1.4**  TripPay’s obligation to pay Commission is conditional on successful receipt of the corresponding Traveller payment and on the Booking becoming a Materialised Booking. |

## 2. Commission calculation

**2.1**  Commission is calculated under clause 4 of the Demand Partner Agreement as the applicable percentage of the Booking Value less the Platform Fee of 1.5% and the Payment Collection Fee of 4.0%.

**2.2**  No further fee is deducted from your Commission. The Platform Fee and Payment Collection Fee are charged to the Accommodation Provider on the Booking Value, not to you on your Commission.

**Worked example on a USD 100.00 Booking Value at 10%:**

| **Line** | **Amount** |
|---|---|
| Booking Value | USD 100.00 |
| Less Platform Fee (1.5%), charged to the hotel | USD 1.50 |
| Less Payment Collection Fee (4.0%), charged to the hotel | USD 4.00 |
| Subtotal | USD 94.50 |
| Commission at 10% of Subtotal, paid to you in full | USD 9.45 |

**2.3**  Commission is calculated on the Booking Value as confirmed at the time of booking and is not reduced where the Traveller shortens the stay after check-in.

## 3. Payout

**3.1**  Commission becomes available for withdrawal within 24 hours of Traveller check-in, or of delivery in the case of an add-on, subject to successful receipt of the Traveller payment and to verification under clause 5.

**3.2**  Amounts shown in your account represent sums owed to you by TripPay. They are not a deposit, an account balance held for you, or stored value. No amount may be added other than by way of Commission earned, and the only permitted use is withdrawal to your registered bank account.

**3.3**  Any amount not withdrawn within 30 days is paid automatically to your registered bank account.

**3.4**  You must provide and maintain accurate payout details, including company details, name, government identification, tax identification and bank account information. Only the designated representative named at registration may change payout details, and any change must be requested in writing.

**3.5**  Payout costs, bank charges and currency conversion costs are borne by you. TripPay settles in USD, EUR or GBP as standard.

**3.6**  Where you provide incorrect payout details, any bank charge arising is deducted from your next payout. Where your account is closed, frozen or unable to receive payment for reasons not attributable to TripPay, TripPay’s payment obligation is discharged provided its records show payment was made.

**3.7**  TripPay may limit the value of a single payout for operational or compliance reasons and make a series of payouts instead.

## 4. Reversals and recovery

**4.1**  Commission is reversed where the Booking is cancelled, refunded, results in a no-show, is not a Materialised Booking, is fraudulent, or was obtained in breach of the Demand Partner Agreement.

**4.2**  Where Commission has already been paid, TripPay may recover it by deduction from future payouts. Any shortfall carries forward until cleared, and TripPay may invoice you where the relationship ends with an amount outstanding.

**4.3**  Chargebacks. Where a Traveller successfully disputes a payment on a Booking attributed to you, the related Commission is reversed.

**4.4**  AI Agent Operators. Where a dispute or reversal arises from a Booking created without proper Traveller authorisation, or from inaccurate presentation of a rate or policy by an AI Agent, you are liable for the full disputed amount and not only the Commission. Such a Booking is not payment fraud and no fraud carve-out applies.

**4.5**  Processing errors. TripPay will correct any payment processing error. Where you receive funds in error you will return them immediately, and TripPay may recover them from future payouts.

**4.6**  You authorise TripPay to recover any amount you owe under these Payment Terms or the Demand Partner Agreement by deduction from future payouts.

**5.4**  You are responsible for your own taxes on Commission received. TripPay may collect tax information and withhold where required by law.
