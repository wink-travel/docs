## Which agreement applies to you

| If you are | Your agreement |
|---|---|
| A traveller booking a stay | [Terms of Service](/terms/) |
| A hotel or other accommodation provider | [Accommodation Provider Agreement](/legal/accommodation-provider-agreement/) and [Accommodation Provider Payment Terms](/legal/accommodation-provider-payment-terms/) |
| A hotel activating the agent-collected channel | The above, plus the [Agent-Collected Channel Addendum](/legal/agent-collected-channel-addendum/) |
| An affiliate, creator, API partner or AI agent operator | [Demand Partner Agreement](/legal/demand-partner-agreement/) and [Demand Partner Payment Terms](/legal/demand-partner-payment-terms/) |
| A travel agent where TripPay collects payment | [Demand Partner Agreement](/legal/demand-partner-agreement/) and [Demand Partner Payment Terms](/legal/demand-partner-payment-terms/) |
| A travel agent collecting payment yourself, as merchant of record | [Travel Agent Agreement](/legal/travel-agent-agreement/) |

The Terms of Service apply to everyone. Where a role-specific agreement conflicts with them, that agreement takes precedence to the extent of the conflict.

## Agreements

### [Terms of Service](/terms/)

The umbrella document. Applies to every user of the platform and contains the booking terms for travellers, including how payment, cancellations and refunds work, and the arbitration agreement and class-action waiver in Section 17 with its 30-day opt-out.

### [Accommodation Provider Agreement](/legal/accommodation-provider-agreement/)

For hotels and other accommodation providers listing supply. Covers supply and rate parity, add-ons, distribution channels, fees and commission, chargebacks with a fraud carve-out, operations, and overbooking and relocation obligations.

### [Accommodation Provider Payment Terms](/legal/accommodation-provider-payment-terms/)

Between the hotel and TripPay Corporation. **Section 1 is the appointment** that makes TripPay the hotel's limited payment collection agent — accepting these Payment Terms constitutes that appointment. Also covers collection, handling of funds, fees, payout, refunds, chargebacks and taxes.

### [Agent-Collected Channel Addendum](/legal/agent-collected-channel-addendum/)

Accepted by a hotel when it activates the agent-collected sales channel in the Extranet. On that channel the travel agent collects payment, neither Wink nor TripPay is in the money flow, and the hotel's recourse for non-payment is against the agent.

### [Demand Partner Agreement](/legal/demand-partner-agreement/)

For affiliates, creators, API partners, AI agent operators, and travel agents where TripPay collects. Common terms in Sections 1–7, with role-specific additions for API partners, creators and AI agent operators in Sections 8, 9 and 10.

### [Demand Partner Payment Terms](/legal/demand-partner-payment-terms/)

How commission is calculated and paid. Section 1 is the mirror of the hotel appointment: the partner does **not** appoint TripPay and never holds guest funds.

### [Travel Agent Agreement](/legal/travel-agent-agreement/)

For travel agents approved to collect payment themselves and act as merchant of record. Approval-gated, subject to a credit limit with automatic decline, with a 1.5% booking fee invoiced monthly. There is no payment collection fee, because TripPay is not collecting.
