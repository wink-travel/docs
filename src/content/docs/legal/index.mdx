*Agreements effective 6 September 2026 · Payment Terms and Platform Agreements Version 1.1 effective 1 October 2026 · Governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, USA*

> The General Terms of Service apply to everyone. If you list rooms, sell rooms, or collect payment yourself, one additional agreement also applies to you — find it in the table below. Two companies contract with you: Winkfluence Inc. is the platform, and TripPay Corporation collects and pays out money. Both are Wyoming corporations.

## Which agreement applies to you

| If you are | Your agreement |
|---|---|
| A traveller booking a stay | [General Terms of Service](/legal/terms/) |
| A hotel or other accommodation provider | [Accommodation Provider Agreement](/legal/platform-agreements/#accommodation-provider) and [Accommodation Provider Payment Terms](/legal/payment-terms/#accommodation-provider) |
| A hotel activating the agent-collected channel | The above, plus the [Agent-Collected Channel Addendum](/legal/payment-terms/#agent-collected) |
| An affiliate, creator, API partner or AI agent operator | [Demand Partner Agreement](/legal/platform-agreements/#demand-partner) and [Demand Partner Payment Terms](/legal/payment-terms/#demand-partner) |
| A travel agent where TripPay collects payment | [Demand Partner Agreement](/legal/platform-agreements/#demand-partner) and [Demand Partner Payment Terms](/legal/payment-terms/#demand-partner) |
| A travel agent collecting payment yourself, as merchant of record | [Travel Agent Agreement](/legal/platform-agreements/#travel-agent) |

The General Terms of Service apply to everyone. Where a role-specific agreement conflicts with them, that agreement takes precedence to the extent of the conflict.

## Agreements

### [General Terms of Service](/legal/terms/)

The umbrella document. Applies to every user of the platform and contains the booking terms for travellers, including how payment, cancellations and refunds work, and the arbitration agreement and class-action waiver in Section 17 with its 30-day opt-out.

### [Platform Agreements](/legal/platform-agreements/)

Three separate agreements on one page, one per role. The **Accommodation Provider Agreement** covers supply, rate parity, add-ons, fees, chargebacks and relocation. The **Demand Partner Agreement** covers affiliates, creators, API partners and AI agent operators, with role-specific additions in Sections 8, 9 and 10. The **Travel Agent Agreement** is for agents approved to collect payment themselves as merchant of record. Each tab states who it is between.

### [Payment Terms](/legal/payment-terms/)

Three separate agreements on one page, one per payment route — the **Accommodation Provider Payment Terms** (the appointment that makes TripPay the hotel's limited payment collection agent), the **Demand Partner Payment Terms** (commission settlement; the partner never holds guest funds), and the **Agent-Collected Channel Addendum** (the travel agent collects and neither Wink nor TripPay is in the money flow).
