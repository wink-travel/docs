Before anything, you need to create a user account on Wink.

<Aside type="note" title="Are you booking a trip?">
This guide is for professionals who want to work with Wink's travel inventory — hoteliers, travel agents, affiliates, and other partners managing bookings, properties, and distribution. If you're a traveler, you don't need an account up front: you'll create one as part of the checkout flow when you make a booking.
</Aside>

Follow these steps:

<Steps>
1. Go to [app.wink.travel](https://app.wink.travel)
2. Click on `Sign-In / Register` in the top-right corner
3. You will be taken to a login form. Click on the `Register` link below the form fields.
4. Complete the registration form and click `Register`. You are redirected to the log in page.
5. Upon login, you will be redirected to our [Portal](/portal/create-account) where you can create your first account.
</Steps>

Creating a Wink user account gives you access to all the features on the platform. You can create unlimited properties and affiliate accounts.
