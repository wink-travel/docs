Welcome to Wink. Whether you run a hotel, promote travel to an audience, book on behalf of
clients, or build software for any of the above — you're in the right place.

Wink was created to make selling travel inventory exciting again, and to make it accessible to
far more people than the handful of large players who used to control it. These docs walk you
through every part of the platform, from creating your first account to going live with a deep
API integration. You don't need to read them in order; head straight to the section that
matches what you're trying to do — and if you already know what you're after, the AI-powered
search field at the top of the page will take you straight to it.

An account on Wink lets you:

- Create and manage properties and sell everywhere potential travelers are.
- Create and manage affiliate accounts and earn a commission on bookings you facilitate.
- Create and manage travel agent accounts and book on behalf of your customers.
- Grab your WinkLinks vanity url (*e.g. https://i.trvl.as/bob*) and use it as your `Link-in-Bio URL`.
- Book your next vacation and save.
- Do all the above as a developer by integrating with Wink.

## About Wink

`The Wink Travel Platform` is a groundbreaking new travel platform designed, from the ground up, to empower hotels, experience providers, influencers, and affiliates alike. By seamlessly integrating innovative tools with a user-friendly interface, Wink transforms the hospitality industry, making it easier than ever to connect, grow, and succeed.

Everything you do as a business now lives in one place — **Portal**. Alongside it sit our
**booking engine**, which is where every booking is ultimately made, and our **developer
tools**, for teams who want to build on top of us. Pick the section below that fits you and
start there.

## What it costs

The vast majority of what Wink offers is free. Listing and managing your property, building
your affiliate network, using the booking engine, publishing your WinkLinks page, writing your
own social posts, analytics — none of that costs anything, and there is no subscription and no
setup fee.

When a booking happens, Wink takes a small percentage to keep the lights on. And a handful of
premium features that cost us real money every time you use them — AI-generated social posts,
automated replies, high-volume API access — are billed as **pay-as-you-go usage fees** at
rates we think you'll find very reasonable. Each of those comes with a free monthly allowance,
so you can try them before you ever pay anything.

## Core concepts

> You see one... you've seen them all

As you familiarize yourself with all of Wink's features, there is plenty of overlap in how things work.
Understanding the core concepts will give you greater familiarity and more confidence as you work through our features.

Here we go:

- Every section usually has a way to list, add, update and remove entries.
- When you're editing an entry, a red asterix <Badge text="*" variant="danger" size="large" /> on a form field means it's required.
- If a specific form field is incorrect, you will see a red border around it with red text explaining what the problem is underneath the form field.
- You will only be able to `Save` a form once the form is valid and some changes have been made to it.
- Some forms encompass multiple tabs, such as the guest room form.
- All tabs need to be valid before you can continue.
- A tab containing missing or invalid form data, will display a yellow triangle <Badge text="⚠️" variant="caution" size="large" /> next to its name.
- When submitting a form, most forms will display a `Confirmation` dialog asking you if you want to continue.

## Portal

[Portal](https://app.wink.travel) is the single app where everything you do as a business
lives. Whatever kind of account you have, this is where your journey starts:

- **Property** — list your hotel, manage rates, content and availability, and get an army of affiliates selling for you.
- **Affiliate** — monetize your audience and earn commission on every booking you send our way.
- **Travel agent** — search bespoke rates and inventory directly from suppliers and book for your clients.
- **Payment** — track your earnings and withdraw your funds, powered by TripPay.
- **Social** — let AI keep your social channels active and converting.
- **Link Manager** — build and publish your WinkLinks page.
- **Settings** — branding, managers, applications, webhooks and payouts, per account.

You can manage as many accounts as you like from a single login, and Corporate is on its way.
