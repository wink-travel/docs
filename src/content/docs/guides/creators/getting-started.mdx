This guide takes you from sign-up to your first bookable hotel link. It takes about 15 minutes. By the end you'll have a live storefront, your first link to share, and you'll know how and when you get paid.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- An e-mail address to register with.
- The link to your main social profile, channel or blog.
- Bank details. These can wait until you've earned your first commission.

## How you earn

- **10% default commission** on every booking you send to a hotel on the Wink Network.
- **Six months of credit per click.** If someone clicks your link today and books three months later, the booking is still yours.
- **No follower minimum** and no application. Every hotel active on the Wink Network is available to you from day one.
- **No guest service to handle.** Payment is collected for the hotel, which stays merchant of record and looks after the guest. You earn the commission.

For the full picture, see [How Travel Creators Earn Commission on Hotel Bookings](/resources/travel-creator-hotel-commissions/) and [Pricing](/pricing/).

## Step 1: Create your Wink login

<Steps>
1. Go to [app.wink.travel](https://app.wink.travel) and click `Sign-In / Register` in the top-right corner.

2. Click the `Register` link below the login form, complete the form and click `Register`.

3. Log in. You land in Portal, ready to create your account.
</Steps>

## Step 2: Create your creator account

Your login is you. Your **account** is the creator profile your audience and hotels see.

<Steps>
1. Click `Accounts` in the left sidebar, then `Create`.

2. Choose **I am an Affiliate**. On Wink, creators are affiliates: you earn commission on the bookings you send.

3. Fill in the form:
   - **Name**: your creator name, as your audience knows you.
   - **Entity type**: `Individual`, unless you work through a company. This decides how you're paid.
   - **Type**: `Influencer`.
   - **Description**: one or two lines about what you post. It shows on your storefront.
   - **Website**: your main profile, e.g. your Instagram, TikTok or YouTube URL. Hotels look here when you ask to work with them directly.
   - **City**: where you're based.

4. Accept the Terms & Conditions and Payment Terms, then click `Save`.
</Steps>

:::tip[Your storefront address]
Your account name sets your storefront address: an account called *Queen Bee* gets `i.trvl.as/queen-bee`. You can edit this part of the address, the slug, later. Slugs are first come, first served, so claim yours early. See [Claim your URL](/portal/link-manager/claim-your-url/).
:::

## Step 3: Set up your storefront

Your storefront is your **WinkLinks** page, a link-in-bio page that also sells hotels. It already exists at your `i.trvl.as` address. Now make it yours.

<Steps>
1. Upload a profile picture and check the description from Step 2.

2. Add the links you already share: your channels, your blog, even other affiliate links. They all live on one page. See [Content](/portal/link-manager/content/).

3. In `WinkLinks > Design`, switch on **Intelligent** so Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X and Spotify links show as playable posts instead of plain links. Pick a layout and your colours. See [Settings](/portal/link-manager/settings/).
</Steps>

Want to see one live? Visit [i.trvl.as/starringmay](https://i.trvl.as/starringmay).

## Step 4: Find hotels worth promoting

Hotels are in **Studio**, the creator side of Portal. There's nothing extra to sign up for.

<Steps>
1. Click `Browse > Hotels` in the main nav bar, or search from your dashboard.

2. Filter by destination, dates and price until you find hotels you'd actually recommend.

3. Keep the good ones: add them to a [curated list](/portal/studio/curated-lists/) (every account starts with `Favorites`), or [save the search](/portal/studio/saved-searches/) to come back to it.
</Steps>

:::tip
Promote places you've stayed at or would stay at. Your audience can tell the difference, and it's what turns clicks into bookings. For ideas, see [Discover Great Deals](/guides/affiliates/discover-great-deals/).
:::

<Steps>
1. On a hotel in your search results, click `Make a link`.

2. Give the link a name you'll recognise, add a title and description, and pick the images people will see when you share it.

3. Choose a theme to show the live price on top of the image.

4. Click `Save`. You get a short link like `trvl.as/3xWCH`, carrying your credit.
</Steps>

Full options are in [Shareable Links](/portal/studio/shareable-links/).

### Which format to use where

| Where you post | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Stories, Reels, TikTok, comments, DMs | [Shareable link](/portal/studio/shareable-links/) | One short link for one hotel, with a price preview. |
| Link in bio | [WinkLinks](/portal/link-manager/wink-links/) | One address for everything you recommend. |
| A themed trip ("5 boutique stays in Lisbon") | [Curated list](/portal/studio/curated-lists/) shown as a [grid](/portal/studio/grids/) or [map](/portal/studio/maps/) | Lets people compare and book from one place. |
| Your blog or website | [Card](/portal/studio/cards/), [grid](/portal/studio/grids/) or [map](/portal/studio/maps/) | Bookable hotels right inside your article. |

## Step 6: Share it

Put your link where your audience already looks: your bio, story link stickers, YouTube descriptions, pinned comments, newsletters and blog posts. Six months of credit means a link in an evergreen video or article keeps earning long after you post it.

:::note[Label your partner links]
Most platforms and many countries require you to show that you earn from a link, for example with *#ad*, *#affiliate* or the platform's paid-partnership label. Follow the rules for your platform and country. It also builds trust with your audience.
:::

## Step 7: Track what's working

Click your profile icon in the top-right corner, then `Analytics`, to see clicks and bookings. Drop what doesn't convert and post more of what does. See [Analytics](/account/analytics/).

## Step 8: Get paid

<Steps>
1. **Add your bank account**: go to `Account > Bank Accounts`, add your account and mark it **Primary**. Automatic payouts go to your primary account. See [Bank Accounts](/portal/settings/payouts/).

2. **Commission becomes available within 24 hours of the guest checking in.** A booking made in March for a stay in June pays out in June.

3. **Payouts go by bank transfer.** Wink sends payouts through Revolut, so if you have a Revolut account, use it: it's the smoothest option. Any other bank account works too.

4. **Transfer fees are paid at cost** and depend on your country and currency. A quote calculator shows the cost before you withdraw. See [Pricing](/getting-started/pricing/) and [Withdraw Funds](/portal/payment/withdraw-funds/).
</Steps>

:::note
As your earnings grow, we may ask you to verify your identity before releasing funds. This is standard for any platform that pays people out.
:::

## Level up

- **Work directly with a hotel.** Once you have 5 bookings on Wink, you can ask a hotel for a direct connection, for example a special deal for your audience or a group rate. With a direct connection, you and the hotel can agree a commission other than the 10% default. See [Network](/portal/studio/network/).
- **Post more with less effort.** [Social](/portal/social/what-is-social/) creates and schedules hotel posts for you. Manual posts are always free; AI-made content is pay-as-you-go. See [Pricing](/pricing/).
