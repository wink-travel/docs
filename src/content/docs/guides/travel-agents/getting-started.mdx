This guide takes a travel agency from registration to its first client booking. It also explains the two ways an agency can work on Wink, so you can choose the right one before you start.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Your company details.
- Your travel agency registration number, or your IATA code if you have one.
- A bank account for your commission.

## Choose how you'll work

| | Standard (most agencies) | Agent-collected payment |
|---|---|---|
| Who takes the client's payment | Collected for the hotel, which stays merchant of record. You enter your client's card at checkout. | You do, as merchant of record. |
| What you earn | **10% default commission** on each booking | The commission in your own agreement with each hotel |
| What you pay Wink | Nothing | **1.5%** of each booking's value, invoiced monthly |
| Paying the hotel | Handled for you | You pay each hotel directly, under your own agreement with them |
| Refunds, chargebacks, payment compliance | Handled for you | Your responsibility |
| Approval | None: start straight away | Wink's written approval, with a credit limit |

Most agencies start on the standard route. Agent-collected payment suits agencies that already collect client payments under their own licence and contract directly with hotels.

## Step 1: Register your agency

<Steps>
1. Go to [app.wink.travel](https://app.wink.travel), click `Sign-In / Register` and register.

2. Click `Accounts` in the left sidebar, then `Create`, and choose **I am a Travel Agent**.

3. Fill in your agency's details. Choose **Company** as the entity type: it decides how you're paid. See [Create Account](/portal/create-account/).

4. If you want to collect payment yourself, say so during registration. This is a request, not an approval. See [Step 6](#step-6-apply-to-collect-payment-yourself).
</Steps>

## Step 2: Add your bank account

Go to `Account > Bank Accounts`, add your agency's account and mark it **Primary**. Commission is paid there by bank transfer. Wink sends payouts through Revolut, so a Revolut business account is the smoothest option, but any bank account works. See [Bank Accounts](/portal/settings/payouts/).

## Step 3: Find hotels for your client

<Steps>
1. Click `Browse > Hotels` in the main nav bar.

2. Search by destination, dates and occupancy, and filter until you have the right options for your client.

3. Save shortlists to a [curated list](/portal/studio/curated-lists/) if you're comparing options with the client.
</Steps>

The rates you see are the hotel's own. Wink passes them through unchanged, with no markup layer: you earn through commission. See [Search](/portal/studio/search/).

## Step 4: Book for your client

<Steps>
1. Choose the room and rate, and enter your client's name and contact details as the traveller.

2. At checkout, enter the card your client gave you for this booking. The payment is collected for the hotel, and the hotel stays merchant of record.

3. Confirm. You, the traveller and the hotel each receive a confirmation e-mail. The booking code starts with `WNK`.
</Steps>

:::caution[Handle your client's card with care]
Ask for the card only for the booking you're making and enter it straight into the checkout. Don't write it down, e-mail it or keep it in your own records.
:::

## Step 5: Manage bookings and get paid

- **Bookings:** click `Bookings` in the main nav bar to see every booking, filter it, export it to CSV, resend a confirmation or cancel within the hotel's cancellation policy. See [Bookings](/portal/travel-agent/bookings/).
- **Commission:** 10% by default. Once you work with a hotel regularly, you can ask it for a [direct connection](/portal/studio/network/) and agree a different commission.
- **When you're paid:** commission becomes available within 24 hours of the guest checking in, and goes to your primary bank account. Transfer fees are paid at cost. See [Pricing](/getting-started/pricing/).

## Step 6: Apply to collect payment yourself

Skip this step if you're happy on the standard route.

<Steps>
1. Request it during registration (Step 1).

2. Wink contacts you at your registered e-mail address and asks for your paperwork: company details, travel agency registration number or IATA code, and anything else needed to assess your application.

3. Wink reviews it, for example your financial standing and regulatory status, and confirms in writing. Approval comes with a **credit limit**: the most you can hold in unsettled bookings at any time.

4. Until you're approved, you can keep booking on the standard route.
</Steps>

Once approved, you take the client's payment yourself and pay each hotel directly. For each booking you deduct, in order, the 1.5% Wink fee and then your commission; the rest is due to the hotel:

| On a USD 1,000.00 booking with a 10% commission | |
|---|---|
| Booking value | USD 1,000.00 |
| Less Wink fee (1.5% of booking value) | USD 15.00 |
| Subtotal | USD 985.00 |
| Less your commission (10% of subtotal) | USD 98.50 |
| **Due to the hotel** | **USD 886.50** |

Wink invoices the 1.5% fee monthly, in USD, payable within 15 days. You're responsible for refunds, chargebacks, payment security (PCI-DSS) and any licence your country requires to collect payment. The full terms are in the [Travel Agent Agreement](/legal/platform-agreements/).

## Connect your own systems

Agencies with their own booking tools can search and book through the [Partner API](/integrations/partner-api/). Travel agency accounts can create bookings directly with it. See the [Partner API reference](/partner-api/partner/).
