This article shows you how easy it is to get your property listed on Google Maps and other Google Hotel digital real estate with Wink.

It's recommended that you've already read:

- [Extranet > Network](/portal/extranet/network/).

Here are the steps to activate the Google channel:

<Steps>
1. Log into your account at https://app.wink.travel.
2. Choose the property you want to work with.
3. Click on Distribution > Explore Network from the main nav bar.
4. Scroll down to `Affiliates`.
5. In the `Name` filter, type in `Google`.
6. Click on the search result by the name of Google.
7. This takes you to the Google performance overview.
8. Scroll to the bottom and click the `Activate` button.
</Steps>

By clicking `Yes`, you create a new sales channel for Google, at 0% commission and using the same channel discount you set for the Wink Network.

:::note
Give Google some time to grab your rates and data. This process is not immediate. Your property should be up and running within a 24 hour period or sooner.
:::

That's it! That's all you need to do to get your property listed on Google Maps and other Google surfaces with Wink.

## Where the guest lands

Google starts the booking flow by forwarding the guest to the Wink Booking Engine on book.wink.travel, fully in [your branding, with your own GA4, Tag Manager and tracking pixels](/portal/settings/branding). The guest arrives with dates, guests, currency and language already filled in. Google traffic does not land on your own domain or on a third-party booking engine.

Wink does not offer a Google feed on its own. We feed Google only where the booking completes on the Wink Booking Engine, because controlling the checkout is how we guarantee that the price Google shows is the price the guest pays.

## Keep the Google price accurate

- Keep rates and availability current, in the [Extranet](/portal/extranet/what-is-extranet/) or through your [channel manager](/guides/hoteliers/channel-managers/).
- Include every mandatory fee and tax in the rate, so the price on Google equals the total at checkout.
- Wink holds your `official site` badge. Switch off any other provider that also holds it for your property.

:::tip[Pro Tip]
This is a free listing. You don't need to, and can't, get better placement on Google by raising the commission on this sales channel.
:::
