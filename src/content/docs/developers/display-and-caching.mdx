The Demand Partner Agreement does not allow price comparison with Wink supply "other than as expressly permitted" (clause 11.3). This page explains how Wink grants that permission for search display, and the rules your display must follow once you have it.

## What search display means

**Search display** is showing hotels from the Partner API in response to a user's search, alongside other hotel offers, including unpaid alternatives from other sources, with a click-out that opens the selected hotel's Wink Booking Engine under your affiliate account.

It covers search, content and click-out only. Creating, changing or cancelling bookings through the Partner API needs Wink's separate written approval.

## Rules for every display

- **Live rates.** Rates and availability must reflect live data at the time of display (clause 8.2).
- **Rates unchanged.** Show each rate exactly as the Partner API returns it, with no markup, surcharge or alteration. The hotel sets and controls its rate.
- **Accurate presentation.** Show prices, inclusions and cancellation policies accurately and without changing their meaning (clause 6.1).
- **Never present Wink as the seller.** Your product stays visually and structurally distinct from Wink, never presents itself as Wink, and never presents Wink as a separate online travel agency or as the seller (clause 7.4). The contract for the stay is between the traveller and the hotel.
- **Disclosure.** Disclose your commercial relationship with Wink where the law requires it (clause 6.2).
- **No onward use.** Don't sublicense, transfer or disclose Wink supply data or content to anyone else (clause 11.3).

## Transient display

Fetch rates and availability from the Partner API for each display. Don't store them after the session ends.

Display that follows these rules is not a "static copy of content" under clause 7.5, nor caching of stale rates under clause 8.2.

## Caching property content

Property content (names, descriptions, images, amenities and policies) may be cached only within the cache semantics published for the Partner API `Content` service, and refreshed in line with them.

## Identifiers you may keep

You may retain these, and only these:

- the Wink property ID and property `urlName`,
- booking reference numbers of bookings attributed to you,
- your own attribution, client and configuration IDs, with the related click and booking dates.

Use them only to de-duplicate hotel listings across sources, and for attribution, reporting and commission reconciliation. Don't retain rates, availability or property content beyond the rules above, and don't use any data from Wink, including these identifiers, to train, fine-tune or evaluate a model, or to build or improve a supply database (clauses 7.5 and 10.6).

## How to request permission

Search display needs Wink's express written permission, given as a side letter to your Demand Partner Agreement. It takes effect once both the letter and the Agreement are accepted, and Wink can withdraw it on notice or straight away if the rules above are broken.
