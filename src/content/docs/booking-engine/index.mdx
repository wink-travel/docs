Booking Engine is the transaction layer underneath every Wink route: a hotel's own website, partner and creator links, consumer discovery and AI agents. Payment is collected on the hotel's behalf, as its agent, so the hotel stays merchant of record.

## The booking flow

- [Search](/booking-engine/search/) — find available inventory.
- [Property Detail](/booking-engine/property-detail/) — rooms, rates, policies and add-ons.
- [Checkout](/booking-engine/checkout/) — a single hotel-branded payment step.
- [Booking Confirmation](/booking-engine/confirmation/) — what the guest receives.

## For the traveller

- [Bookings](/booking-engine/bookings/) — manage an existing reservation.
- [Bucket List](/booking-engine/bucket-list/) — save properties for later.
- [Travel Preferences](/booking-engine/travel-preferences/) — personalise results.
