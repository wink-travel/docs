  <p class="text-muted-foreground mt-4 text-pretty text-lg">

    social content, partner demand, consumer discovery, AI handoffs, developer integrations, Booking
    Engine fulfillment, and payment. Hotels use Wink to control what can be sold, and at what cost. Partners use
    Wink to create qualified booking demand. Developers and platforms build hotel commerce into other
    products. The platform charges 5.5% all-in on confirmed bookings, with no setup fee and no monthly
    platform fee.

<section class="mx-auto w-full max-w-3xl px-4 py-16">
  <h2 class="font-heading text-3xl font-semibold tracking-tight @xl:text-4xl">Where Wink came from.</h2>
  <div class="text-muted-foreground mt-6 space-y-5 text-pretty @lg:text-lg">
    <p>
      Direct distribution for independent hotels has been broken for two decades. Hotels handed the
      guest relationship to OTAs in exchange for reach, and the cost compounded — 15–25% per booking,
      no guest email, no next booking unless the OTA brought them back. Wink was built to change that
      structure, not to add another channel on top of it.
    </p>
    <p>
      The platform connects hotel-controlled supply to every demand route that matters today: hotel
      websites, social content, partner storefronts, QR campaigns, consumer discovery through
      Traveliko, AI assistant handoffs, and developer-powered platform integrations. Booking Engine
      fulfills every route from the same layer, at 5.5% all-in with no setup fee and no monthly
      platform fee.
    </p>
    <p>
      Wink operates across Southeast Asia and beyond, with a growing panel of independent and boutique
      hotels using the platform to build direct demand outside the OTA default. The company is
      headquartered in Bangkok.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>
