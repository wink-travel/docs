## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They let a site remember your actions and preferences over time. We also use similar technologies — such as local storage, pixels and software development kits — and we refer to all of them as "cookies" in this policy. Cookies may be **session** cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or **persistent** cookies (which remain until they expire or you delete them), and **first-party** (set by Wink) or **third-party** (set by a provider we use).

## 2. The categories we use

- **Strictly necessary** — required to operate the Platform and provide services you request, such as signing in, keeping your session secure, load balancing and completing a booking or payment. These cannot be switched off through our controls.
- **Functional / preferences** — remember choices such as your language/locale and display preferences to improve your experience.
- **Analytics / performance** — help us understand how the Platform is used (for example, which pages are visited) so we can measure and improve it. We use Google Analytics for this.

We do **not** use advertising or cross-context behavioral-advertising cookies. **[CONFIRM no advertising/retargeting tags are present, and that this remains true if marketing pixels are added later.]**

## 3. Cookies we use

The table below describes the main cookies and similar technologies we use. Exact names and durations may vary as providers update their products. **[CONFIRM the exact cookie names, providers and retention periods with a cookie scan before publishing.]**

| Cookie / technology | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Session / authentication | Strictly necessary | Keeps you signed in and your session secure on the platform | Session |
| Security / CSRF | Strictly necessary | Protects forms and requests against cross-site attacks | Session |
| Language / locale preference | Functional | Remembers your selected language across 40+ locales | Persistent |

| Cal.com (demo scheduling) | Functional | Supports demo-booking scheduling when you book a call | Per Cal.com |

## 4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by the providers we use to run the Platform, including **Google** (Google Analytics, Firebase Hosting), **Stripe** (payments at checkout), **Cal.com** (demo scheduling) and **Cloudinary** (image delivery). These providers process data under their own privacy and cookie policies; please review them for details. See also our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) Section 8 for the providers we work with.

## 5. How to manage cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

- **Our cookie controls** — where a cookie banner or preferences tool is shown, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies and change your choice at any time. **[CONFIRM whether a consent-management banner is in place; if you serve the EEA/UK, prior opt-in consent for non-essential cookies — including Google Analytics — is generally required before they load.]**
- **Browser settings** — most browsers let you block or delete cookies and warn you before they are set. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the Platform from working.
- **Google Analytics opt-out** — you can install Google's [opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) to prevent your data being used by Google Analytics.

## 6. "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers offer "Do Not Track" (DNT) or **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signals. **[CONFIRM how Wink responds to DNT/GPC signals]** — where required by applicable law (for example, California), we treat a recognized GPC signal as a valid request to opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our use of cookies changes. We will post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date.
