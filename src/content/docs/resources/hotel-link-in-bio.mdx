## Why "link in bio" matters for hotels

Social platforms limit clickable links. Instagram, TikTok and similar profiles give a brand essentially **one link** to work with — so the "link in bio" became the doorway between a hotel's content and everything it wants a follower to do next. The same constraint shows up behind QR codes, in short campaign URLs, and in partner recommendations: one link has to carry the moment.

Generic link-in-bio tools solve the wrong half of the problem. They organize links — but they send people **away** to other pages, show no live hotel inventory, and can't take a booking. For a hotel, that's a leak: attention arrives, then disappears to a homepage or an OTA.

The fix is to treat the single link as a **storefront**, not a directory.

## Link-in-bio vs a Travel Storefront

| | Generic link-in-bio | Travel Storefront |
|---|---|---|
| What it holds | A list of URLs | Bookable hotel cards, offers, lists, maps |
| What it does | Sends people away | Moves people toward a booking |
| Inventory | None | Live hotel inventory and rates |
| Checkout | None | Booking Engine fulfillment |
| Attribution | None or basic clicks | Preserved through the booking |
| Built for | Desktop afterthought | Mobile-first |

A Travel Storefront looks like a link-in-bio page — clean, mobile, one link — but works like a bookable shopfront.

## What a hotel Travel Storefront can hold

One public link can present a whole menu of bookable content:

- **Bookable hotel cards and offers** — rooms, packages, seasonal deals.
- **Curated lists** — "best for families," "wellness escapes," event hotels.
- **Maps and grids** of bookable supply.
- **Rich content, files and media** — brochures, welcome info, galleries.
- **QR destinations** — the storefront behind in-property codes.
- **Normal links** — for everything that isn't a booking.

This is why a Travel Storefront works equally well for a property's Instagram bio, a wedding room block, a destination campaign, or a partner's recommendation page.

## How it works on Wink

- **[Studio](/products/studio/) builds the assets.** Create bookable cards, links, maps, grids and QR codes from approved hotel supply.
- **[WinkLinks](/products/winklinks/) is the storefront.** Collect those assets under one mobile-first public `i.trvl.as/your-name` link — in card view or list view. It's **free to use**.
- **[Booking Engine](/products/booking-engine/) fulfills the booking.** A tap moves into checkout — payment, confirmation, the whole stay — with source and partner attribution preserved.

It's the closing surface of [hotel social commerce](/resources/hotel-social-commerce/): content creates attention, the storefront holds the bookable options, and Booking Engine converts.

## Who uses it

- **Hotels** — one link for the social bio, QR codes, email and in-property collateral, showing rooms, offers, dining, spa and experiences.
- **Partners and creators** — a destination page, event collection or campaign storefront built from network hotels, with [10% default commission and 6-month attribution](/partners/).
- **Events** — a room block or destination storefront attendees can book from one link.

## A practical setup

1. **Claim your link** — set up `i.trvl.as/your-name`.
2. **Build assets in Studio** — the rooms, offers and collections you want to feature.
3. **Arrange the storefront** — choose card or list view and lead with your strongest bookable offers.
4. **Put the link everywhere** — social bios, QR codes, email signatures, campaign URLs, partner pages.
5. **Keep it current** — swap in seasonal offers and events so the storefront always reflects what you're selling.
6. **Measure** — see which entries and sources convert, and refine.

## Common mistakes

- **Using a generic link-in-bio that only lists URLs.** It sends followers away instead of toward a booking.
- **Linking the bio straight to a homepage.** Lead with bookable offers, not a landing page.
- **Letting the storefront go stale.** Update offers and events so it stays worth the click.
- **Forgetting partners.** The same storefront model lets partners present your hotels with attribution intact.
