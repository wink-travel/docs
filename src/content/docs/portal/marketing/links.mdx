Shareable links is a core feature of Wink and it rose out of the need to communicate inventory and pricing in a dynamic way through the simplest way possible on the Internet; namely the hyper link.

<h4 align="center"> [https://trvl.as/3xWCH](https://trvl.as/3xWCH) </h4>

Above is an example of one of our links. It's short and portable... even short enough to remember. Though short, this link packs an **explosive** amount of information, such as:

- Your App ID.
- Your Affiliate ID.
- The image(s) and text to display to the user.
- The look-n-feel (*i.e. theme, colors and more*).
- The itinerary to use for our price query: the dates and guests set in the link's [Customization](/portal/settings/branding/#itinerary) (*by default, today for 1 night with 2 guests*).
- The currency to display price in.
- The language to display text in.

The rest of this article walks you through how to create, customize and share your links, and how a link turns into a card.

Above is an image taken from [Browse hotels](/portal/marketing/browse-hotels) and shows you some of the things you can do with your search results. One of those actions is `Make a link`.
Click on that button and it will redirect you to our link form page where you can start customizing your link.

You can also start a link from:

- `Marketing > Links`, where hotels have a `Create link` button that makes a link to their own property.
- A master rate that is on sale: use `Create link` in the rate card's actions (`Rates > Master rates`). The link opens on that room type and rate plan.

`Make a link` is disabled for a hotel until it has an active sales channel.

## Customize link

 The form lets you customize your link in the following ways:

<Steps>
 1. Give it a name for you to remember what the link is about.
 2. Select the [Customization](/portal/settings/branding) you want to apply to this link. Its [Itinerary](/portal/settings/branding/#itinerary) sets the dates and guests your link opens with, so set your default dates there.
 3. Add keywords, separated by a comma, that will be used by [Web Crawlers](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Glossary/Crawler).
 4. Optionally enter you X account handle or Facebook App ID.
 5. Add titles and descriptions in the languages you want the user to see.
 6. Select one, or more, images that will show when you share the link on platforms such as Facebook or LinkedIn.
 7. Choose a theme / overlay that adds a branded design on top of the image(s) you selected. *Note: This is how you get prices to display on top of the image.*
 8. Click the `Save` button to continue.
 </Steps>

Above shows you an image with all the available actions for your link:

- **Preview** Shows you a quick overview of how the link was configured and what it will look like to the user.
- **Update** Updates your link configuration.
- **Add to Link manager** Adds the link to your Link manager account.
- **Copy** Copies the link to your clipboard so you can easily share it anywhere you want (*including [Link manager](/portal/link-manager/what-is-link-manager) *).
- **Share** Opens up a new window with options to share on many social networks and messaging platforms.
- **QR code** Generates a QR code that you can share on platforms like IG that are more image-focused.
- **Embed** Shows you how to embed this link as an [Ad Banner](/integrations/web-components/#content-loader) into your website.
- **Use with WordPress** Shows you how to use our [WordPress plugin](/integrations/wordpress) to embed this link into your website.

How to save your QR code:

- If you are on your laptop, right click on the image and select to `Save image`.
- If you are on your mobile phone:
    - Press down and hold your finger on top of the image until an option appears to save the image to your Photos.
    - Take a screenshot of the QR code, crop it to fit, and save to Photos.

### Embed

```html {3, 6-9, 11, 13-16}
<html>
  <head>
    <link rel="stylesheet" href="https://elements.wink.travel/styles.css"></link>
  </head>
  <body>
    <wink-content-loader
       layout="AD_BANNER"
       id="3xWCH"
    ></wink-content-loader>

    <script src="https://elements.wink.travel/elements.js" type="module" defer></script>

    <wink-app-loader
      client-id="YOUR CLIENT ID GOES HERE"
      configuration-id="YOUR CUSTOMIZATION ID GOES HERE"
    ></wink-app-loader>
  </body>
</html>
```

Here is how to embed your link, as an ad banner, into your site:

- Line 3 shows you how to embed the Wink styles into your site.
- Lines 6 through 9 shows you how to use the [wink-content-loader](/integrations/web-components/#content-loader) Web Component and tell it to fetch an ad banner for your code.
- Line 11 shows you how to embed our Javascript into your site.
- Line 13 shows you how to embed the [wink-app-loader](/integrations/web-components/#application-loader) Web Component and tell it to fetch your page-level configuration preferences.

Before you publish, replace the two placeholders on lines 14 and 15:

- `YOUR CLIENT ID GOES HERE`: the client ID of one of your applications. Open your profile menu in the top-right corner and select `Applications`. Add your website's domain to that application's `Redirect URIs`, so your visitors can be authenticated.
- `YOUR CUSTOMIZATION ID GOES HERE`: the ID of the [Customization](/portal/settings/branding) to apply.

## Links become cards

A link on its own is a short URL that opens the booking engine. It becomes a **card** once you either embed it on your website with our [Web Components](/integrations/web-components/#content-loader) or add it to Link manager. There is no separate card to build: the same link, shown inside a page, is a card.

A card represents a single supplier (e.g. hotel or experience provider) or inventory (i.e. room type, meeting room, spa etc) and exposes the details via an interactive, bookable, card UI. A traveler can interact with a card in the following ways:

- Click the `Book` button.
- Click the `Gallery` link to see all photos and videos for this inventory.
- Click `Best deal` link, if available, to see the best priced room.
- Click `Hotel details` link, if available, to see property information.

A card supports multiple faces to minimize its size and to categorize the data, making it easily consumable and giving you a greater chance to convert the traveler to a booking. A card has either two or three faces:

- A hotel card shows property data on the front and the best room type on the back. The price, on both sides, displays the best price for the hotel.
- A room type card shows room type data on the front and property data on the back. The price displays the price of the room type on the front and the best price for the hotel on the back.
- All other cards (e.g. spa, restaurant etc) have 3 faces. Example: Spa data displays on the front with the best price of the spa. Room type data for the best priced room is on the second side. Property data along with the best priced room is on the third face.

To embed a link as a card, use `Embed` on the link and the `layout` of the card you want, for example `GUEST_ROOM`:

```html
<wink-content-loader
  layout="GUEST_ROOM"
  id="2de7ee9c-61c9-11ef-9722-42004e494300"
></wink-content-loader>
```

The surrounding page setup is the same as in the ad banner example above.
