You'll be asked for the contact details of your reservation desk during setup.

To manage them, click on `Property > Contact Info` in the main navigation tab.

## Reservations contact

The primary contact for booking-related questions. This is required.

## Revenue and marketing contacts

Optional contacts for revenue and marketing inquiries. Check `Same as reservations` for either one if the same person or team handles it — otherwise fill in separate contact details.

## Hours of operation

Set a start and end time for your reservation desk. This lets guests know when they can expect a live response.

:::note
It's not required to use a specific name of the main person at your front desk. It just adds to the familiarity of the place.
Incoming guests would love to connect with a real person upon arrival and be able to ask something like "Hi... is Lisa here?"
:::
