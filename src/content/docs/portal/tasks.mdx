Tasks is where Wink surfaces hints on what you could improve about your account — a checklist of optional, high-impact steps, with a completion ring showing how far along you are.

Currently available for property accounts. There is no menu item for it: the **Setup guide** card appears in the bottom-right corner of Portal on desktop screens for as long as the selected property has outstanding tasks. It shows your completion percentage and the next step to take.

- Click the expand button, or the `Next:` link, to open the full task list.
- Click the close button to hide the card. It is hidden for the current session only — reloading Portal, or switching to another account, brings it back.
- Once every task is complete the card no longer appears.

## What shows up

Hints are grouped by theme, and only appear while there's something to do — once a group is complete, it drops off the list:

- **Facilities** — Meeting rooms, restaurants, spas.
- **Area** — Activities, attractions, points of interest.
- **News-worthy** — Awards, publications, third-party reviews.
- **Awareness** — Social connections, Green Index score.
- **Upsells** — Promotions, add-ons.

Click any hint to go straight to the form where you can complete it.
