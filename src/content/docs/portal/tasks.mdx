Tasks is where Wink surfaces hints on what you could improve about your account — an always-visible checklist of optional, high-impact steps, with a completion ring showing how far along you are.

Currently available for property accounts, reached via `Tasks` in the Extranet section of the main nav bar.

## What shows up

Hints are grouped by theme, and only appear while there's something to do — once a group is complete, it drops off the list:

- **Facilities** — Meeting rooms, restaurants, spas.
- **Area** — Activities, attractions, points of interest.
- **News-worthy** — Awards, publications, third-party reviews.
- **Awareness** — Social connections, Green Index score.
- **Upsells** — Promotions, add-ons.

Click any hint to go straight to the form where you can complete it.
