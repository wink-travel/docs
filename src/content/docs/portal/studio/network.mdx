When you first get started as an affiliate on Wink, you find some stuff you want to sell on the platform and share it on social media, your travel blog, app etc.
At some point, you feel that you've become quite proficient with our tools and you have some bookings under your belt.
The next thing you can do to level up your selling game is to seek out direct relationships with suppliers.

:::note
You can interact with your active and pending relationships by clicking on `Partnerships` in the Studio section of the main nav bar — active connections live under `Established`, requests you're waiting on live under `Pending`.
:::

This article covers when and how to ask a supplier for a direct relationship that could be beneficial to both of you.

## When to ask

Keep in mind, for most affiliates, it doesn't make any sense to connect directly with a supplier. Most suppliers already offer up their best deals and upsells to everyone on the platform.
However, we recommend that you do attempt to initiate a direct connection with one of our suppliers when any of the following conditions are met:

- You have a large audience of potential bookers that fit particularly well with a specific supplier.
*E.g. You're a ski bum with some sick powder, back country, ski content and all your followers are ski bums. Connecting with a ski lodge to ask for a unique mid-week deal or similar is smart.*
- You bring your own group of travelers with you to a destination.
*E.g. You organize yoga retreats and a group of 20 yogis are confirmed. With a direct connection, the retreat could give you a unique promo code you can share with your fellow practitioners that entitles them to a group discount.*
- You're a travel agent or corporation dealing with a large volume of bookings.
*Both travel agents and corporations are considered to be affiliates on Wink but they also have their own dedicated sites*

## Requirements

There is no booking minimum: you can ask any hotel for a direct connection. Two rules apply:

- **One request per hotel.** While a request is pending, or once a connection is active, you can't send another request to the same hotel.
- **An introductory message.** Your request must include a short message, which is sent to the hotel with it.

Hotels are busy, so we still recommend building a track record on Wink before you ask.

## How it works

When you've found a hotel you'd like to work with (see [Search](/portal/studio/search/)), here's what happens:

<Steps>
1. Open the actions menu on the hotel's card and choose `Request a direct connection`. The option is greyed out when you already have a direct connection with that hotel.
2. A form asks you to introduce yourself to the hotel. Please be courteous and stick to the point: who you are, why you are reaching out, how you can be of service to them, and any specifics around `the ask` that you have at this time.
3. Click `Send request` and wait for their response. This could take anywhere from one day to a week. Hotels are busy, so please be patient.
</Steps>

The hotel will respond with:

- **Accept** Your request was accepted and a direct sales channel between the hotel and you is created. The hotel can configure it in many different ways (see [Benefits](#benefits)).
- **Reject** Your request was rejected and you keep access to the same inventory as before, through the Wink Network. You can re-apply 90 days after the rejection. In the meantime, only the hotel can start a direct connection with you.
- **Non-responsive** For whatever reason, the hotel does not respond at all. At this point, you should consider looking for another hotel.

## Benefits

Until a hotel accepts you, you sell it through the Wink Network, on the channel discount and commission the hotel set for the Wink Network. A channel discount is the discount a hotel gives a sales channel, which the channel uses to display discounted rates.

Once you've been accepted, the hotel can set the following on your new sales channel:

- Its own channel discount for your channel.
- A bespoke commission for you.
- Deals that are only available through your channel.
- Inventory and upsells that are only available through your channel.
