:::caution
Grids can no longer be created in Portal. Lists replaced them: a [curated list](/portal/studio/curated-lists) or [saved search](/portal/studio/saved-searches) can be embedded on your site or added to WinkLinks directly, with no grid step in between.
:::

Open `Marketing > Lists` from the main nav bar and pick the `Curated`, `Saved searches` or `Destinations` tab to find your lists.
