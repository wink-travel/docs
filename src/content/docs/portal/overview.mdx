[Portal](https://app.wink.travel) is home base. One login, one app, and everything you need to turn travel into your business — whether that means filling rooms, building an audience, wiring up a channel manager, or just dropping a link in your bio.

## What Portal lets you do

- **Properties** — List your rooms, dial in rates and availability, sync your calendars, and watch the bookings roll in. Hotels, chains, brands, management companies — this is your command center.
- **Affiliates** — Turn your audience into income. Promote Wink's travel inventory and earn a commission on every booking, whether you're a general affiliate, a travel agent booking for clients, a social-media creator, or a corporate/group account.
- **Integrators** — Plug in and sync up. Channel managers, PMS, and CRS providers connect through Portal to keep rates and availability flowing in real time, no manual updates required.
- **Content creators** — Don't want an account? Grab a free WinkLinks page instead — a vanity URL, organized links, and a home for everything you want to share.

Pick your lane above and dive in — Portal grows with you as you do more.
