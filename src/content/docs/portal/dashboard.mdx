The dashboard is the first thing you see when you open [Portal](https://app.wink.travel) — your account's command center. Every account type sees the same two sections; only the underlying data differs depending on whether you're managing a property or an affiliate-type account (Studio, Travel Agent, Corporate).

## Activity

A quick glance at your booking health, with cards showing:

- Total bookings
- Average sale value
- Total sales amount

A link takes you straight to your full bookings list for more detail.

## Analytics

A chart tracking your sales over time, with a link through to your full analytics for deeper filtering and date ranges.
