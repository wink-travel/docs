By creating an [affiliate account](/portal/marketing/what-is-marketing), you already have a Link manager vanity url. Link manager publisher features exist in Marketing, alongside our other affiliate features.

### Example

If your account name is Queen Bee, your Link manager vanity url will be:

<h4 align="center">https://i.trvl.as/queen-bee</h4>

The part after `i.trvl.as/` is called the slug. It starts out as your account name, and you can edit it later. Slugs are available on a `first come - first serve` basis - Grab your unique URL while you can!
