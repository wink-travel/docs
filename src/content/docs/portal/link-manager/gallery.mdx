The Gallery is your account's media catalog: every picture and video you have added to Wink in one place. Open it from `Link manager > Gallery` in the main nav bar. It is available to hotels and affiliates that can manage content.

## Browse your media

- Assets are listed newest first. Click `Load more` to see older ones.
- Use the `Type` and `Origin` filters to narrow the list.
- A dot on an asset marks it as AI-enhanced or AI-generated. Hover over the dot to see which.

The Gallery is read-only for now: you can't upload, delete or edit assets from this page.

## Reuse media instead of uploading it again

Wherever you attach a picture or video, `Choose from gallery` sits beside `Upload`. Pick an asset that is already in your catalog and it is attached without uploading it a second time. You'll find it in:

- the social [post](/portal/social/posts) editor, for images and videos separately,
- the media panel of a Link manager [entry](/portal/link-manager/content),
- the `Upload file` dialog when you add a file to Link manager.
