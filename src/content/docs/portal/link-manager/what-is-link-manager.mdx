Link manager, also called a **link-in-bio**, lets you save and organize URLs you like and share them from a single page. Your browser bookmarks are a simple example. With the advent of social media, there came a greater need for exposing and making links available to your friends and audiences, and a single link in your profile bio became the way to do it.

We built [Link manager](https://i.trvl.as/) to be the link-in-bio for the travel industry. It's here to inform, excite, advertise... and lets you book great deals from hotels all over the world.

## Features

- Link manager lets you add all your existing affiliate links, like Commission Junction and Amazon, for free.
- You get a cool vanity url, such as [https://i.trvl.as/bob](https://i.trvl.as/bob), for those times when you can only share a single URL.
- You can organize and share links and upload files.
- Hotels, chains & brands can use Link manager as an advanced booking engine:
    - Supports multiple properties.
    - Supports multiple destinations.
    - Supports multiple currencies.
    - Supports multiple languages.
- Link manager supports `rich content` *(see below)*.
- Go from IG to book in just 2 clicks 🚀

## Rich content

:::note
Set `Intelligent` to 🟢 in your [Settings](/portal/link-manager/settings) to use this feature.
:::

If you share a link from a site that supports `rich` / `oEmbed` content, your link comes alive on Link manager.

Supported sites:

- Facebook - *Interact with post and play a video*.
- Instagram - *Interact with post and play a video*.
- YouTube - *Play a video*.
- X (formerly Twitter) - *Interact with a post*.
- Spotify - *Play your favorite playlists*.
- Wink - *See `Advanced`*.

## Advanced

Use Link manager to sell your:
