Notifications is a global feature — it isn't tied to any one section. Click the bell icon in the top-right corner, next to Settings, to open a panel with every notification across all the accounts you manage.

## Using the panel

- A badge on the bell shows your unread count (capped at `99+`).
- Switch between `Unread` and `All` to filter the list.
- Click a notification to jump straight to what it's about.
- Dismiss individual notifications you no longer need.

When you have none, the panel shows an empty state instead of a list.
